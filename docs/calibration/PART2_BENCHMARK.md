# Part 2 benchmark — Open cloze

Status: Phase 8.5A baseline

## Official format rule

Cambridge defines one modified cloze with eight gaps. Each answer is one word and carries one mark. The stated focus is awareness and control of grammar with some vocabulary. See the [official format page](https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/qualifications/advanced/format/) and [handbook](https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/Images/167804-c1-advanced-handbook.pdf).

## Observed official exemplars

The two official sample texts measured 153 and 151 running words excluding gap markers. This is an observed benchmark, not a published word-count rule. Density is about 19 running words per scored gap.

The task depends on connected-text grammar: reference, linking, complementation, prepositions, auxiliaries, determiners and clause relations. A good item cannot be reduced to eight unrelated fill-in-the-blank sentences.

## Current corpus audit

The three seed texts contain 95, 95 and 90 running words: roughly 39% shorter than the observed mean. Their 11–12 words per gap make the texts noticeably denser and give less discourse context.

One approved item accepts both `must` and `can` for the same gap. Multiple accepted answers are not automatically wrong, but each variant needs explicit semantic equivalence and must not change the proposition. The current validator checks membership, not whether variants are equally defensible.

Severity: **HIGH** for under-length and insufficient semantic adjudication.

## Empirically derived internal target

- 140–175 running words with exactly eight one-word gaps.
- A coherent titled text with discourse context around each gap.
- Planned structural variety; no overconcentration on simple relative pronouns or obvious prepositions.
- No adjacent/near-adjacent gaps that turn prose into a grammar worksheet unless strongly justified.
- Every accepted variant must preserve grammar, register and meaning in the full text.

## Critic rubric and hard rejection

Reject if a second unlisted word fits naturally, accepted variants alter meaning, punctuation gives away the form, the item is solvable below C1 through a trivial local cue, or the passage is not coherent without the gaps. The critic must read the reconstructed full text for every accepted answer set.
