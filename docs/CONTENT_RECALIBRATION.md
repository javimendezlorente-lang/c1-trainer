No candidate, generated item, or downloaded content enters rotation without schema, structural, grading, duplicate, semantic and human review gates.

The current repository treats the 24 seed fixtures as original, human-reviewed fallback material calibrated to the internal empirical bands. This is not a claim that Cambridge imposes these word counts, nor a claim that the texts are official Cambridge material. Phase 8.5 generation remains dormant.
